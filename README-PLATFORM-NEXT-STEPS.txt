THE GESTURE CO. PLATFORM FOUNDATION

This version adds the user-interface foundation for:
- Customer login
- Customer order dashboard
- Order statuses and delivery tracking area
- Invoice download area
- Company employee management area
- Admin dashboard
- Product management and image upload interface

IMPORTANT: The login, invoice buttons and admin controls are currently visual prototypes. Before real customers use them, they must be connected to a secure backend.

4. Products database and image storage
